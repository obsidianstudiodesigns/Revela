Cookie policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This page explains how cookies and similar technologies are used when you visit the Revéla website.

Our own cookies

The Revéla website does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies, and we do not run analytics that profile you.

Third-party services

Some parts of the page are provided by other companies, which may set cookies or collect technical data under their own policies:

  • Google Maps shows the map to our salon. Google may set cookies when the map loads. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts and unpkg deliver the typefaces and icons used on the site. They receive your IP address in order to deliver these files.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and may log basic technical information for security.
  • WhatsApp and Facebook links take you to those platforms, where their own cookie policies apply.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The website still works, although the embedded map may not load.
