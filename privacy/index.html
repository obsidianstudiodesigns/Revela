Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Revéla ("we", "us") respects your privacy. Hair loss can be personal, and we treat everything you share with us with discretion. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

  • Details you give us when you book or enquire: your name, phone number, email address and the service you're interested in.
  • Information about your hair and scalp that you choose to share so we can recommend a suitable solution. This may include health-related information, which we treat as special personal information.
  • Before and after photographs, only with your consent.
  • Basic technical information collected by our website host and by embedded services such as Google Maps and Google Fonts (for example your IP address and browser type).

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and to book, confirm or change appointments.
  • To recommend, make, fit and maintain your hair solution.
  • To keep records of the products and services you've received.
  • To share before and after photos on our website or social media, only if you have given consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time.

The booking form and WhatsApp

Our website booking form does not store your details. When you press "Send on WhatsApp", your message opens in WhatsApp, where you choose whether to send it. Messages sent through WhatsApp are handled under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it when needed to provide your service (for example with a supplier making a custom hair system, without unnecessary personal details), or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep client records only for as long as needed for your care, maintenance appointments and our legal obligations, after which they are securely deleted.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to its use, or withdraw consent. Contact us using the details above. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to keep your information safe, including limiting who can access client records.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
