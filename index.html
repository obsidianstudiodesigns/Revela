Questions, answered

Something else on your mind? Message Jeanné privately.

Does it hurt?

No. Every application is pain-free and non-surgical. There's no recovery time and you see the result immediately.

Will people be able to tell?

Each piece is matched to your colour and density, and cut into your own hair. Jeanné will even blend in grey for the most realistic finish, so it looks natural and virtually undetectable.

Can I wash, swim and style it?

Yes. Hair replacement systems can be washed, curled, straightened, tied up and worn swimming. Toppers clip in and out, so you can take them off at night.

How long does it last?

With proper care and professional products, a hair replacement system can last up to two years, with maintenance every 8 to 12 weeks. Toppers can be reused for up to four years, and frontals for 1.5 to 2 years.

I bought my system elsewhere. Can you still help?

Absolutely. Jeanné installs, maintains, cleans, refits, cuts and blends existing systems for men and women.

What does it cost?

Every solution is customised, so you'll get a personalised quote after your consultation. Affordable maintenance and weekly blow-wave packages are available.

Do you see clients in Cape Town?

Yes. Jeanné offers private consultations in the Durbanville area on selected dates. Bookings are limited, so message her to find out when she'll be there next.
